Grand Egyptian Museum

The largest archaeological museum in the world

About the Museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) is one of the most important cultural projects in the world. Located near the Giza Pyramids, it showcases thousands of artifacts from ancient Egypt, including the complete collection of King Tutankhamun for the first time.

Basic Information

  • Location: Giza, near the Pyramids
  • Opening: 2023 (Partial opening)
  • Area: 500,000 square meters
  • Famous For: Full Tutankhamun Collection
  • Architect: Heneghan Peng Architects

Top Attractions Inside

  • Complete Tutankhamun Collection
  • Grand Staircase with Statues
  • Khufu Solar Boat Museum
  • Interactive Digital Exhibits

Did You Know?

  • The GEM is the largest museum dedicated to a single civilization.
  • It is located just 2 km from the Giza Pyramids.
  • The museum includes advanced digital and interactive displays.
