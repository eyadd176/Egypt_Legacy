Nubian Museum

Discover the rich heritage of Nubian civilization

About the Museum

The Nubian Museum in Aswan showcases the history, culture, and traditions of Nubian civilization. It was established to preserve Nubian heritage after the construction of the High Dam, which led to the relocation of many Nubian communities.

Basic Information

  • Location: Aswan
  • Opened: 1997
  • Famous For: Nubian culture and artifacts
  • Type: Cultural & Archaeological Museum
  • Award: UNESCO Award for best museum design

Top Attractions Inside

  • Nubian statues and artifacts
  • Traditional Nubian houses models
  • Ancient tools and daily life items
  • Open-air museum and gardens

Did You Know?

  • The museum won a UNESCO award for its architectural design.
  • It preserves the culture of Nubian people displaced by the High Dam.
  • It includes both indoor and outdoor exhibition areas.
