Hurghada Museum

A modern museum on the Red Sea coast

About the Museum

Hurghada Museum combines ancient Egyptian artifacts with modern exhibitions, offering a unique cultural experience near the Red Sea.

Basic Information

  • Location: Hurghada
  • Opened: 2020
  • Famous For: Mixed exhibitions

Top Attractions Inside

  • Ancient Egyptian artifacts
  • Modern displays
  • Interactive exhibits

Did You Know?

  • It is Egypt’s first museum in partnership with the private sector.
  • It connects tourism with culture on the Red Sea.
