Egyptian Museum

The heart of ancient Egyptian history in Cairo

About the Museum

The Egyptian Museum in Cairo is one of the most important museums in the world. It contains more than 120,000 artifacts from ancient Egypt, including statues, jewelry, mummies, and treasures of the famous king Tutankhamun.

Basic Information

  • Location: Tahrir Square, Cairo
  • Established: 1902
  • Architect: Marcel Dourgnon
  • Famous For: Tutankhamun Collection
  • Number of Artifacts: 120,000+

Top Attractions Inside

  • Golden Mask of Tutankhamun
  • Royal Mummies Room
  • Ancient Jewelry Collection
  • Statues of Pharaohs

Did You Know?

  • The museum was opened in 1902.
  • It houses the largest collection of Pharaonic antiquities.
  • It was the home of Tutankhamun's treasures for decades.
