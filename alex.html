Alexandria National Museum

Discover Alexandria through history

About the Museum

The Alexandria National Museum presents artifacts from ancient, Greco-Roman, and modern Egyptian history in a historic palace.

Basic Information

  • Location: Alexandria
  • Opened: 2003
  • Famous For: Greco-Roman artifacts

Top Attractions Inside

  • Ancient statues
  • Roman artifacts
  • Modern Egyptian history items

Did You Know?

  • The building was originally an Italian-style palace.
  • It covers multiple historical periods.
