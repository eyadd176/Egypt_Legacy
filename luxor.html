Luxor Museum

A window into the treasures of ancient Thebes

About the Museum

Luxor Museum is one of Egypt’s finest museums, located on the east bank of the Nile. It displays a carefully selected collection of artifacts from ancient Thebes, offering a clear and elegant presentation of Egypt’s history.

Basic Information

  • Location: Luxor (East Bank of the Nile)
  • Opened: 1975
  • Famous For: Well-preserved statues and artifacts
  • Type: Archaeological Museum
  • Collection: Artifacts from ancient Thebes

Top Attractions Inside

  • Statues of Amenhotep III
  • Artifacts from Tutankhamun's tomb
  • Mummies of Pharaohs
  • Temple reliefs and sculptures

Did You Know?

  • Luxor Museum is known for its clean and modern display style.
  • It focuses on quality rather than quantity of artifacts.
  • It is located near the famous Luxor Temple.
