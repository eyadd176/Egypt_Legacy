Explore the story of Egypt from ancient to modern times

About the Museum

The National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC) is the first museum in Egypt dedicated to presenting the full history of Egyptian civilization from prehistoric times to the present day. It offers a unique journey through Egypt’s cultural heritage.

Basic Information

  • Location: Fustat, Cairo
  • Opened: 2017
  • Famous For: Royal Mummies Hall
  • Type: Civilization Museum
  • Exhibits: Ancient, Coptic, Islamic, and Modern Egypt

Top Attractions Inside

  • Royal Mummies Hall
  • Main Exhibition Hall
  • Textile Gallery
  • Interactive Displays

Did You Know?

  • The museum hosted the Royal Mummies Parade in 2021.
  • It is the first museum in Egypt to cover all historical periods.
  • The Royal Mummies Hall contains over 20 royal mummies.
