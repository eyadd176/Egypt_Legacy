Imhotep Museum

Dedicated to the genius architect of ancient Egypt

About the Museum

The Imhotep Museum is located in Saqqara near the Step Pyramid of Djoser. It is dedicated to Imhotep, the architect of the first pyramid in Egypt. The museum displays artifacts discovered in Saqqara, highlighting ancient Egyptian engineering and design.

Basic Information

  • Location: Saqqara, Giza
  • Opened: 2006
  • Famous For: Step Pyramid artifacts
  • Type: Archaeological Museum

Top Attractions Inside

  • Statues of Imhotep
  • Artifacts from the Step Pyramid complex
  • Ancient tools and architectural models
  • Stone carvings and inscriptions

Did You Know?

  • Imhotep was one of the first known architects in history.
  • The museum is located near the Step Pyramid of Djoser.
  • It highlights early Egyptian engineering achievements.
